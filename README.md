# 🚀 Low on Power

_A minimalistic precision lander made in 2h 58m for [Trijam #318](https://trijam.net/)_

**By TheEpicSmoking and Dellolio-gilberto**

---

## 🎮 About the Game

In **Low on Power**, your only goal is simple: 
**Land your tiny spacecraft gently.**

But there's a twist: your thruster doesn’t *help* — it *overhelps*. Every tap of the spacebar sends you flying with more force than expected. As the game progresses, your thruster gets stronger and harder to manage. Not better — just *more*.

---

## 🧠 Gameplay Concept

- **Controls**: Tap `Space` to boost — that’s it.
- **Challenge**: The thruster grows stronger each level.
- **Strategy**: The less you press, the better you perform.
- **Resources**: Each level gives you less fuel.
- **Goal**: Land softly, save fuel, and move fast.

You’ve got **three lives** to go as far as you can.  
Your **score** is based on:
- Fuel left
- Time taken to land

**Fly with intention.** Sometimes the best move is *no move at all*.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Tap `Space` to apply thrust.
2. Land softly on the platform.
3. Save fuel, survive, and go further.
